# Legal Disclaimer & Responsible Use

`WiFi-Evil-Twin` is a security research and education tool. It builds a rogue
access point ("Evil Twin") and a captive portal to demonstrate how easily
credentials can be harvested on untrusted networks.

## You may ONLY use this tool:
- On wireless networks that **you own**, or
- On networks where you have **explicit, written authorization** to perform
  security testing.

## You may NOT use this tool:
- On any network you do not own or lack permission to test.
- For any unlawful, malicious, or privacy-violating purpose.
- To capture credentials of people who have not consented to the test.
